Starting a PokeHub work session.

1. Read `docs/PROGRESS.md`. Read `VISION.md` **only** if the task ahead needs a design
   decision — it is long, and CLAUDE.md already carries the rules.
2. Tell me in 3-5 lines: where the project stands, what "Next up" says, and any gotcha
   that applies to it.
3. Propose the single next task, then wait for me to confirm before writing any code.
